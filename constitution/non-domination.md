# Non-Domination

Non-domination is the condition in which an actor is not structurally exposed to unchecked arbitrary power over important capabilities.

Actual interference is not required. A dependency can be dominating when another actor retains unilateral capacity to alter rules, access, visibility or continuity without meaningful recourse.

Power Dynamics operationalizes non-domination through four broad counterpowers:

1. **Verification** — can affected actors inspect what happened?
2. **Contestation** — can they challenge it?
3. **Substitution** — can another mechanism, provider or branch perform the function?
4. **Exit** — can they leave while preserving enough assets and continuity for exit to be credible?

Non-domination does not require complete independence. It requires dependencies that remain governable rather than arbitrary.
