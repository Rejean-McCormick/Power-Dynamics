# Federation and Subsidiarity

Federation coordinates independently governed nodes through shared contracts rather than requiring a flat or centerless architecture.

Subsidiarity places decisions at the lowest level capable of handling them effectively while preserving necessary coordination.

The repo rejects `federation = no center`. Strong local hubs, shared standards, and central reference services can coexist with local sovereignty if substitution and independent operation remain meaningful.
