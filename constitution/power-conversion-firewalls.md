# Power Conversion Firewalls

A **power conversion firewall** is a rule or architectural boundary that prevents legitimate power in one domain from automatically becoming authority in another.

Canonical examples:

```text
Expertise ≠ Sovereignty
Wealth ≠ Votes
Reputation ≠ Truth
Brand ≠ Legitimacy
Technical Control ≠ Political Authority
Execution ≠ Mandate
Narrative Influence ≠ Epistemic Authority
Infrastructure ≠ Ownership of Users
```

Firewalls do not forbid all conversion. Expertise can influence advice; money can fund infrastructure; brand can attract attention. They require the conversion to pass through an explicit mechanism with a visible mandate rather than happening by social or technical default.

A firewall fails when, for example, an advisory score becomes practically binding because institutions stop considering other readings, or when control over the release infrastructure allows maintainers to redefine governance without a separate authorization path.
