# Counterpowers, Legibility, Provenance, and Auditability

A minimum counterpower package is:

> **Verification + Contestation + Substitution + Exit**

Power should also be legible enough that affected actors can identify the relevant holder, mechanism, mandate, inputs, outputs, version, and appeal path.

**Provenance** preserves where claims, artifacts, decisions, and transformations came from. **Auditability** allows independent reconstruction or verification.

Transparency is not sufficient by itself. A transparent system that cannot be contested or substituted can remain dominating. Complexity is not inherently illegitimate either; the problem is complexity functioning as an unanswerable source of authority.
