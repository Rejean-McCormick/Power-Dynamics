# Strong Capability, Weak Sovereignty

This is the repo's compressed constitutional thesis.

A system may legitimately become extremely capable: it may preserve large memories, route complex work, discover expertise, translate across languages, verify artifacts or coordinate many institutions. Capability itself is not the problem.

The danger appears when capability becomes **terminal dependence** — when users cannot meaningfully inspect, contest, substitute, leave, fork or continue without the operator.

Thus the design target is:

> **High functional capability + low arbitrary sovereignty.**

This principle rejects both extremes: weak decentralization that cannot accomplish anything, and powerful centralization that users cannot escape.
