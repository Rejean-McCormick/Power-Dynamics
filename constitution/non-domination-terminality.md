# Non-Domination and Non-Terminality

Non-domination means more than absence of current interference. A relationship is problematic when an actor remains exposed to discretionary power it cannot meaningfully verify, contest, substitute, or exit.

The repository extends this into **non-terminality**: capture of one institution, branch, deployment, or maintainer should not automatically make the broader ecosystem unrecoverable.

`Instance Capture ≠ System Capture` is therefore a design objective, not a guarantee.
