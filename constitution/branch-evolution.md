# Constitutional Branch Evolution

A stable kernel protects the ability to diverge without losing all comparability.

The stable layer may include provenance formats, identifiers, versioning, signatures, interoperability contracts, or minimum audit requirements. Branches may diverge in policies, criteria, ontologies, interfaces, rankings, governance, and implementation.

**Branch mobility** measures whether an alternative can become consequential. A branch may technically exist but remain irrelevant if it cannot be found, imported, supported, recognized, funded, deployed, or integrated.

The goal is:

> **stability at the level of evolutionary capacity, diversity at the level of implementation**
