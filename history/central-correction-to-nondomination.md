# From Central Correction to Non-Domination

Some legacy economic proposals imagine very strong global coordination, automated enforcement, sanctions, and monetary controls.

Later current-core texts emphasize semantic sovereignty, local operation, auditability, bounded expertise, multiple readings, federation, recourse, and non-domination.

The evolution can be summarized as a movement from:

> **find a sufficiently just center to correct the system**

toward:

> **design institutions so that even useful centers remain bounded, contestable, substitutable, and non-terminal**.

This is a major constitutional evolution and should remain explicit in the corpus history.
