# From Dependency to Empowerment

`Abuser l'humanité` diagnoses how debt, time capture, bureaucracy, justice cost, desire, work, and procedural complexity can shrink effective possibility.

`Hacking Reality` develops the opposite movement: language, symbols, alliances, learning, narrative, and organization can expand possibility.

kOA later formalizes the transition as infrastructure: transform dispersed knowledge and commitment into governed collective action and reusable memory.

Power Dynamics therefore inherits both halves:

> **How systems shrink effective possibility**

and

> **How systems generate effective possibility**.
