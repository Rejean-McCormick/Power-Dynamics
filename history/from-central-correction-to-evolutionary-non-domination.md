# From Central Correction to Evolutionary Non-Domination

Earlier proposals often assume that sufficiently transparent, technically enforced and ethically directed central mechanisms can correct inequality or institutional failure. Later work increasingly asks a different question: what if the corrective institution itself becomes a dependency?

The answer shifts toward portability, multiple readings, self-hosting, local sovereignty, recourse and branch alternatives.

This is not a simple reversal. Strong coordination remains valuable. The evolution is toward **constitutionalizing strength**: powerful functions should exist, but their scope, evidence, recourse and replacement paths must remain explicit.
