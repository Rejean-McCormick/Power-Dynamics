# Founder Power Across Phases

A possible maturity trajectory is:

1. founder sovereignty;
2. explicit constitutional constraints;
3. maintainer pluralism;
4. institutional federation;
5. protocol-level continuity.

The goal is not to erase authorship. It is to ensure that independent reproduction, operation, interpretation, and branching do not depend on unique founder permission or inaccessible knowledge.
