# Branch Mobility and Replacement

Branch mobility is the capacity of a demonstrably useful alternative to become consequential.

A healthy ecology allows alternatives to gain visibility, import artifacts, preserve provenance, attract contributors, operate independently and interoperate where useful. The dominant branch may absorb an innovation, coexist with it, merge, or lose dominance.

Replacement is therefore not a constitutional crisis. It is one possible outcome of ordinary evolution.

The pathological case is when alternatives may technically exist but cannot become visible or viable because the incumbent controls identity, distribution, infrastructure, standards or reputation portability.
