# Recombination and Replacement

Evolution is not always replacement.

Branches may coexist, specialize, merge, import specific improvements, recombine into a new branch, decline, or be replaced.

A healthy ecosystem preserves enough lineage to explain how later branches inherit or reject earlier decisions. Replacement should not erase history.
