# Recombination and Evolution

Evolution is richer than competition. Branches can recombine.

A branch may preserve another branch's semantic model while adopting a different reader policy. A third may merge the strongest parts of two credibility models. A local implementation may contribute an improvement back to the shared kernel.

Lineage should therefore represent merges and partial inheritance, not only forks.

The objective is cumulative institutional learning: diversity generates experiments, provenance keeps them comparable, and recombination prevents every improvement from requiring permanent fragmentation.
