# Lineage and Competing Kristals

Kristals can preserve divergent claims, evidence, scopes, policies, and contexts without forcing immediate universal convergence.

Two Kristals may each claim better representation of a phenomenon. The system should preserve sources, assumptions, validation policy, scope, uncertainty, lineage, added evidence, and unresolved disagreement.

A later Kristal may synthesize, specialize, or challenge both. The objective is cumulative epistemic evolution rather than winner-take-all truth declaration.

Branch plurality does not imply epistemic equivalence. A branch can be preserved because it is culturally meaningful, institutionally adopted, experimentally useful, adversarial, or fictional while still carrying weaker evidentiary support than another branch. Lineage should make both **difference of worldview** and **difference of support** visible.
