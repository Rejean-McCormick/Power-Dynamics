# Lineage and Competing Kristals

Competing representations are useful only when their differences remain intelligible.

A branch should preserve lineage: what it derived from, what evidence changed, what assumptions changed, and which disagreements remain unresolved. Kristal-style provenance enables a community to say not merely "ours is truer" but *why* it claims improvement.

The purpose is neither forced convergence nor equal treatment of all claims. It is structured comparison under explicit provenance.
