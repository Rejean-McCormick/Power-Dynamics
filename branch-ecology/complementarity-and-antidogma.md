# Complementarity and Anti-Dogma

Complementarity exists when several branches jointly provide more value than a single universal branch.

Scientific, legal, historical, local, experiential, or cultural representations may answer different questions. Credibility systems can specialize in academic, practical, or community evidence.

Complementarity is not relativism. Branches remain comparable on explicit dimensions.

Dogma, in this framework, is repetition protected from meaningful comparison, revision, or added value. Useful plurality contributes new evidence, context, method, ontology, implementation, translation, or function.

> **No branch should become final merely because alternatives cannot be meaningfully formed, compared, supported, or adopted.**
