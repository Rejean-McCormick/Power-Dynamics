# Three-Axis Model

Every Power Dynamics analysis should locate a phenomenon on three axes.

## Axis 1 — Ontological state

`resource → potential → capacity → puissance → power relation → effect → return`

## Axis 2 — Dynamic

Formation, mobilization, composition, conversion, return, reinvestment, compounding, concentration, stacking, capture, counterpower, diversification, branch evolution, decay, transfer, succession.

## Axis 3 — Domain

Economic, monetary, political, epistemic, semantic, informational, reputational, attention, network, technical, infrastructural, administrative, operational, narrative, symbolic, brand, cognitive, mnemonic, allocation, certification, property, territorial, and others.

A media organization can therefore be strong in distribution and agenda power while a state is stronger in legal enforcement. “More powerful” without a domain and mechanism is incomplete.
