# Imperfection and Measurement

Power Dynamics assumes that socially consequential properties such as competence, ethics, credibility, contribution, legitimacy and even the best available representation of a contested reality cannot be measured perfectly.

The correct model is therefore not:

```text
person → true competence score
```

but:

```text
observed evidence
→ interpretation policy
→ scoped reading
→ uncertainty / limitations
→ use under governance
```

For example:

```text
Competence
≠ evidence of competence
≠ policy interpreting that evidence
≠ resulting score
≠ institutional use of the score
```

The same applies to ethics. A system can represent observable conduct, declared norms, peer evidence or policy-defined indicators. It cannot legitimately convert those observations into an infallible measure of a person's moral worth.

This limitation is not a reason to abandon structured judgment. It is a reason to expose scope, criteria, provenance, time, uncertainty, alternative readings and appeal paths.

Multiple readings are therefore a constitutional feature rather than an embarrassment. A raw democratic reading and an expertise-sensitive advisory reading can coexist. Different Koali distributions can evaluate credibility differently. Different Kristals can represent competing or complementary claims.

The goal is **governable approximation without pretending to finality**.
