# Imperfection, Readings, and Plurality

Power Dynamics assumes that consequential human and institutional judgments are imperfect.

Competence, ethics, credibility, legitimacy, truth, branch quality, and social value cannot be captured perfectly by one score, authority, or implementation. This is not a reason to avoid evaluation. It is a reason to make evaluation **explicitly governed**.

`phenomenon ≠ evidence ≠ policy interpreting evidence ≠ reading/score ≠ institutional use of that reading`

This permits multiple branches or readings without requiring relativism. A branch may be better supported on one dimension and weaker on another. Different Kristals may preserve different evidence, contexts, ontologies, or scopes.

The objective is not perfect governance but **governable imperfection**.
