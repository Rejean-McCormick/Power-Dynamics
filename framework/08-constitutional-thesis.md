# Constitutional Thesis

The central constitutional problem is:

> **How can an infrastructure distribute enormous puissance without becoming sovereign over the puissance it distributes?**

The answer is not decentralization in one dimension. It requires scoped authority, separation of functions, conversion firewalls, provenance, verification, contestation, substitution, exit, revocability, reversibility, local operability, federation, branch mobility, founder decentering, and non-terminal dependency.

The repository's shorthand is:

> **Strong distributed puissance. Weak sovereignty.**

A system can have a strong hub, useful defaults, recognized brands, maintainers, registries, and standards. The issue is whether those centers can silently convert functional centrality into unchallengeable cross-domain control.

> **Architecture is governance before governance is written.**
