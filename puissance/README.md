# Forms of Puissance

**Puissance** is effective mobilizable capacity. The categories below describe what an actor or system can actually do before asking whose possibilities it changes.

## Agency and cognitive puissance

Ability to choose, refuse, learn, reason, synthesize, model, plan, create, and evaluate. Education, time, tools, memory, information quality, and AI can expand it.

## Collective and associative puissance

Capability generated by coordination: finding collaborators, forming groups, mobilizing networks, and acting together. This is a central object of kOA.

## Economic and organizational puissance

Ability to mobilize financial/material resources and to sequence work, allocate effort, coordinate roles, maintain continuity, and deliver outcomes.

## Technical and infrastructural puissance

Ability to build, modify, operate, audit, reproduce, repair, deploy, and continue technical systems. Shared infrastructure can amplify distributed capability without necessarily owning it.

## Epistemic and semantic puissance

Ability to create, structure, validate, compare, preserve, translate, contest, and evolve knowledge, categories, ontologies, and relations of meaning.

## Communicative, reach, and distribution puissance

Ability to make information understandable, transmissible, discoverable, and deliverable across geographic, linguistic, institutional, or network boundaries.

## Operational and mnemonic puissance

Ability to turn orientation into tasks, routing, escalation, execution, closure, and reusable memory. It makes collective action cumulative rather than episodic.

These forms can compose. They become **power relations** only when their exercise materially changes another actor's access, options, standing, visibility, allocation, authority, dependency, or ability to act.
