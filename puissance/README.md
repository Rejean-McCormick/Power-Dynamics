# Forms of Puissance

This directory names the **positive capability side of the Power Show**.

**Puissance** is effective mobilizable capacity. Before asking who controls whom, Power Dynamics asks what an actor, group, institution, or infrastructure can actually do.

## Agency and cognitive puissance

Choose, refuse, learn, reason, synthesize, model, plan, create, evaluate, and use governed AI assistance.

## Epistemic and semantic puissance

Produce, structure, validate, compare, translate, preserve, contest, and evolve knowledge and meaning.

## Collective, network, and coordination puissance

Find people, combine distributed expertise, form groups, coordinate roles, synchronize action, and create capability no participant possessed alone.

## Deliberative and decision puissance

Compare alternatives, preserve reasons, incorporate advisory expertise, and choose through explicit procedures.

## Operational and administrative puissance

Route work, assign responsibility, manage timing, escalate, execute, review, and close loops.

## Mnemonic puissance

Preserve provenance, decisions, actions, failures, outcomes, and lineage so the next cycle starts from reusable memory.

## Economic and allocation puissance

Mobilize material resources, funding, productive assets, and allocation mechanisms.

## Technical and infrastructural puissance

Build, inspect, modify, test, reproduce, repair, deploy, self-host, and maintain systems that lower future coordination costs.

## Distribution, brand, and affiliation puissance

Transmit knowledge, make people or projects discoverable, create public recognition, affiliation, and reach across communities and languages.

## Federation, exit, and evolutionary puissance

Operate independently, substitute dependencies, fork, gather support, preserve interoperability, recombine, and replace dominant branches.

These forms can compose. They become **power relations** only when their exercise materially changes another actor's access, options, standing, visibility, allocation, authority, dependency, interpretation, or ability to act.
