# Network, Association, and Reach Power

Network power is the capacity generated by access to people, institutions and channels of coordination. Association power is the ability to form groups and compose different skills into higher-order capabilities. Reach power is the ability to cross geographic, linguistic or institutional boundaries.

Networks compound because one relationship can unlock others. They can also concentrate because central brokers become unavoidable. Federation and multilingual design can increase reach while reducing the need for one central owner of the network.
