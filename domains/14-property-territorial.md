# Property and Territorial Power

Property and territorial power arise from enforceable control over scarce physical assets, land, facilities, infrastructure locations, housing, intellectual property, or jurisdictional access.

This domain is less specific to current kOA software than other domains, but it remains necessary in a general systems theory of power because digital capability ultimately depends on material substrates.

## Conversion chains

```text
property → exclusion / access → economic leverage
physical infrastructure → service continuity → dependency
IP rights → licensing conditions → technical / economic leverage
jurisdiction → legal authority → operational constraints
```

## kOA relevance

Power Dynamics should track whether supposedly distributed systems remain materially dependent on a small set of datacenters, institutions, domains, signing credentials, trademarks, or physical operators.

Formal software openness does not eliminate material bottlenecks.
