# Technical, Infrastructural, and Cognitive Power

Technical power includes control of code, releases, schemas, deployment and maintenance. Infrastructural power arises when other actors depend on those technical systems to preserve essential capabilities. Cognitive power includes the analytical, generative and predictive capacity supplied by AI or specialized expertise.

The central kOA design claim is that cognitive capability should not automatically receive institutional authority. AI may propose, summarize or detect patterns while binding transformations remain governed by explicit rules, provenance and accountable roles.

For real audits, this domain must descend to signing keys, registries, defaults, update channels, permission models and hosting dependencies.
