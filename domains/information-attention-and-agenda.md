# Information, Attention, and Agenda Power

What is visible enters the field of possible action. Discoverability therefore precedes formal decision power.

Ranking, feeds, search, moderation, defaults and recommendation mechanisms shape which proposals are encountered, which experts are found and which issues become actionable. SmartVote explicitly frames discoverability as governance because what rises is more likely to be cited, funded, learned, adopted and built.

Power analysis should separate popularity, credibility and ranking logic. A system may preserve one-person-one-vote while exercising enormous agenda power through what reaches the ballot in the first place.
