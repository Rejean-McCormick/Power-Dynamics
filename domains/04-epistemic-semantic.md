# Epistemic and Semantic Power

Epistemic puissance is the ability to produce, validate, compare, preserve, and reuse knowledge. Semantic puissance is the ability to structure concepts, categories, relations, translation, and ambiguity.

## kOA mechanisms

- Kristal;
- provenance and lineage;
- trust roots / reader policies;
- SemantiK Architect;
- SenTient;
- multilingual structures;
- competing and complementary Kristals.

## Power relations

- validation power;
- canonization power;
- classification power;
- framing power;
- admissibility power;
- trust-root power;
- apparent-consensus power;
- provenance-obscuration power.

## Core insight

> **To govern meaning is already to govern the collective.**

Definitions affect what can count as harm, evidence, competence, fairness, or action-worthy knowledge.

## Counterpower

Versioning, provenance, alternative readers, competing representations, local semantic governance, portability, and contestation keep epistemic capability from becoming ownership of truth.

A further requirement is **causal-lineage legibility**: source count, citation count, credentials, popularity, and institutional presentation should not be treated as independent evidence when they derive from the same origin or cannot be verified.
