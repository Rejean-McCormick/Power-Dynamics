# Information, Attention, and Media Power

Information and media puissance includes the ability to publish, package, search, rank, recommend, moderate, synchronize, and amplify signals.

## Relational powers

- visibility power;
- discoverability power;
- agenda power;
- moderation power;
- framing power;
- attention power.

Konnaxion matters because discoverability is not merely convenience. What becomes findable can become reusable, fundable, discussable, and politically salient.

## Risk

A system can preserve formal freedom of speech while controlling practical visibility.

## Counterpower

Explicit discovery policies, alternative ranking/discovery layers, portability, federation, transparency, and branch discoverability reduce terminal control of attention.
