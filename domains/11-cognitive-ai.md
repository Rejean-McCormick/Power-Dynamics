# Cognitive and AI-Mediated Power

Cognitive puissance is the effective ability to reason, synthesize, model, plan, generate, compare, interpret, and learn.

AI can multiply cognitive throughput, especially when combined with structured knowledge, provenance, memory, and workflow.

```text
AI + Kristal + policy + human judgment
→ higher cognitive throughput
```

## Risks

- hidden framing;
- silent prioritization;
- opaque synthesis;
- model dependency;
- AI-generated defaults becoming policy;
- cognitive convenience becoming hidden sovereignty.

## Principle

> **AI assists under governance; it does not silently acquire governance.**

The key question is not whether AI is powerful. It is whether its cognitive amplification remains subordinate to explicit authority and reconstructible evidence paths.
