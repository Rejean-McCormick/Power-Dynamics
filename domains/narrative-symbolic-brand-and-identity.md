# Narrative, Symbolic, Brand, and Identity Power

Narrative makes complex systems transmissible. Symbols compress meaning, identity and affiliation. Brands can convert recognition into attention, trust, commerce and network growth.

These are real powers, but they should remain separated from epistemic and political authority. A compelling founder, costume, logo or story can help people enter a system without becoming evidence that the system is correct.

Identity also includes privacy and optionality. Information not yet disclosed can preserve freedom of movement and a future option to reveal. Once mass recognition occurs, some forms of ordinary anonymity may be irreversible.
