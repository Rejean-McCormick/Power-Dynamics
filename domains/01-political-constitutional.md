# Political and Constitutional Power

Political / constitutional power concerns standing, legitimacy, mandate, representation, agenda, decision rights, veto, amendment, jurisdiction, and the authority to bind collective action.

## kOA power surface

kOA can increase political puissance by making publics better able to frame issues, discover evidence, compare proposals, expose advisory readings, make decisions, route implementation, and preserve institutional memory.

The canonical chain is:

> **Meaning → Knowledge → Visibility → Credibility → Agenda → Deliberation → Decision → Execution → Memory**

This makes political power partly **pre-political**: control of categories, discovery, ranking, and credibility can shape what reaches formal decision.

## Main relational powers

- agenda power;
- decision power;
- mandate power;
- veto / amendment power;
- legitimacy power;
- administrative implementation power;
- precedent power.

## kOA safeguards

- political equality as baseline;
- expertise as bounded advice rather than sovereignty;
- explicit lenses and multiple readings;
- scoped authority;
- auditability and recourse;
- branch mobility and institutional alternatives.

## Key conversion risks

```text
credibility → political authority
money → political standing
technical administration → policy control
narrative popularity → legitimacy
```

These are precisely the kinds of conversions Power Conversion Firewalls are meant to expose.
