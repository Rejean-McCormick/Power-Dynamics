# Narrative, Symbolic, and Brand Power

Narrative puissance makes complex systems memorable, transmissible, culturally legible, and capable of affiliation. Brand puissance can convert recognition into attention, trust, distribution, commerce, or coordination.

## Conversion chains

```text
narrative → attention → affiliation → mobilization
brand → recognition → merchandise / funding → infrastructure
symbol → memory → identity → network
```

## Relational risks

- charisma replacing procedure;
- brand becoming truth;
- symbolic centrality becoming founder worship;
- trademark control becoming protocol control.

## Ethical self-binding

The source fiction explicitly publishes a living ethical code, vigilance against monopoly and domination, recognition and shared merit, self-mockery, criticism, revision, and non-dogmatic transmission.

This is treated as **narrative self-binding**: the channel that creates symbolic power also carries public constraints on that power.

Narrative is legitimate as **function, not sovereign**.
