# Power Domains

Domains answer **where** resources, capability, puissance, and relational power operate.

Version 6 separates domains that were previously grouped too loosely, especially:

> **Political ≠ Economic ≠ Monetary**

The distinction matters because the mechanisms, mandates, risks, and counterpowers differ.

## Domain rule

Avoid statements such as “X is more powerful than Y” without specifying:

> **powerful in what domain, through what resource, capacity, relation, mechanism, duration, conversion path, and counterpower environment?**

## Cross-domain analysis

The most important questions often concern transitions between domains:

```text
knowledge → credibility
credibility → visibility
visibility → agenda
money → infrastructure
infrastructure → dependency
brand → affiliation
administration → de facto policy
```

See [`../framework/10-cross-domain-power-conversion.md`](../framework/10-cross-domain-power-conversion.md).
