# Evolutionary Power and Counterpower

Evolutionary puissance is the practical ability to improve, fork, substitute, recombine, or replace systems and institutions.

This is not merely emergency resistance to corruption. It is a normal mechanism of institutional experimentation.

```text
imperfection / opportunity
→ criticism
→ variation
→ demonstration
→ comparison
→ support
→ coexistence / merge / replacement
→ further variation
```

## Core counterpowers

> **Verification + Contestation + Substitution + Exit**

## Branch mobility

Formal forkability is insufficient. A better branch must be able to:

- preserve data and provenance;
- remain interoperable;
- gather maintainers and support;
- become discoverable;
- operate independently;
- influence or replace a dominant branch.

The goal is **improvement without final authority**.
