# Administrative and Operational Power

Administrative puissance is the ability to route, assign, schedule, validate, escalate, record, and close work. Operational puissance is the ability to turn decisions into coordinated execution.

## Orgo surface

```text
decision
→ task
→ owner
→ routing
→ deadline
→ escalation
→ action
→ closure
→ memory
```

## Relational powers

- assignment power;
- timing power;
- approval power;
- escalation power;
- closure power;
- workload power.

## Political significance

Administration can change outcomes without formally changing policy. Therefore:

> **Execution ≠ mandate redefinition.**

Scoped roles, audit trails, appeal, revocability, and explicit decision-to-work boundaries are constitutional requirements.
