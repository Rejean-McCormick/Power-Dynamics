# Resources and Stocks

Resources are inputs, not power by definition. A stock becomes politically significant only through the capacities, conversions, dependencies, and relations it supports.

## Material, financial, and time resources

Money, property, energy, equipment, housing, physical infrastructure, and time are foundational because many other capacities depend on them. Time is especially political: formal rights can be unusable when work, debt, bureaucracy, care burdens, or procedure consume the time needed to exercise them. Financial resources are unusually convertible because they can purchase labor, infrastructure, attention, expertise, legal support, mobility, or time.

## Knowledge, expertise, and memory

Knowledge can be shared without consuming the original resource, which makes it strongly generative. Expertise is not identical to credentials or reputation; it includes accumulated practice, methods, understanding, and evidence. Memory includes decisions, reasons, failures, outcomes, and operational traces. When made reusable, memory lowers the cost of future action.

## Networks, reputation, and brand

Relationships, trust, reputation, affiliations, and brand recognition can lower coordination cost and open pathways to attention, collaboration, funding, and institutional access. They are not automatically authority. These resources are especially prone to positive feedback and incumbency effects.

## Technical, data, and infrastructure

Code, schemas, hardware, APIs, data, keys, registries, protocols, and operational knowledge can be small artifacts with large systemic effects. Their significance depends on custody, reproducibility, substitution, recovery, and whether others must pass through them.

## Identity, rights, and optionality

Identity, privacy, credentials, legal rights, licenses, and preserved choices condition future action. Optionality is valuable because action has not yet been irreversibly committed. A private identity or unpublished corpus can preserve future timing and strategy. Optionality is potential until it becomes effectively usable.
