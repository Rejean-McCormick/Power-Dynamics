# Literature Roadmap

Future external research should compare the framework with at least:

- Robert Dahl and Steven Lukes on dimensions of power;
- Hannah Arendt on power as acting in concert;
- Michel Foucault on productive and infrastructural power;
- Philip Pettit on non-domination;
- Pierre Bourdieu on economic, social, cultural and symbolic capital;
- Albert Hirschman on exit and voice;
- Elinor Ostrom on polycentric governance and commons;
- Crozier & Friedberg and resource-dependence theory on organizational power;
- Bowker & Star on classification and infrastructure;
- Yochai Benkler and platform-governance scholarship on networked power;
- Miranda Fricker on epistemic injustice;
- Stafford Beer, Ashby, Forrester and Meadows on feedback and system dynamics.

This file intentionally names a research agenda rather than importing unsourced claims into the current source-grounded framework.
