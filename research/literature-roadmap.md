# Literature Roadmap

This is a roadmap for future external research, not a claim that the listed thinkers endorse the Power Dynamics framework.

Potential interlocutors include:

- Dahl and Lukes — dimensions of power;
- Foucault — dispersed power, dispositifs, classification, conduct;
- Arendt — power through acting in concert;
- Pettit — non-domination;
- Bourdieu — economic, social, cultural, symbolic capital;
- Crozier and Friedberg — zones of uncertainty;
- Pfeffer and Salancik — resource dependence;
- Ostrom — polycentric governance and commons;
- Hirschman — exit and voice;
- Bowker and Star — classifications and infrastructure;
- network theorists — centrality, brokerage, diffusion;
- Ashby, Beer, Meadows, Forrester — cybernetics and system dynamics;
- Fricker — epistemic injustice;
- platform governance scholarship — moderation, ranking, infrastructure and dependency.

Future work should ask: **what does each theory explain that Power Dynamics misses, and what does Power Dynamics connect that each theory usually treats separately?**
