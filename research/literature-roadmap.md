## Next research priorities

1. **Domain-scoped expertise** — expand empirical literature on transfer limits, calibration, forecasting, and expert-selection mechanisms.
2. **Protocol / standards governance** — standards bodies, Internet ossification, backwards compatibility, migration governance, and interface control.
3. **Metric gaming** — Goodhart/Campbell effects, reputation markets, adversarial optimization, sybil resistance, and cumulative advantage.
4. **Epistemic democracy** — expertise, political equality, cognitive diversity, deliberation, and institutional design.
5. **Platform power** — ranking, moderation, recommender systems, identity, app stores, payment rails, and infrastructural dependency.
6. **Digital commons** — Ostrom-inspired governance of software, data, compute, schemas, models, and shared registries.
7. **AI-mediated authority** — when model outputs, defaults, summaries, or agentic execution become hidden decision rights.

Future work should continue asking:

> **What does each theory explain that Power Dynamics misses, and what does Power Dynamics connect that each theory usually treats separately?**
