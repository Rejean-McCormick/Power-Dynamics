# Contributing

Contributions should preserve both faces of the repository:

1. **Power Show clarity** — make concrete what a capability enables;
2. **Power Dynamics rigor** — distinguish capability from the relations of power created around it.

## Before adding a concept

Ask four questions:

1. **What becomes possible?** What practical capability does the mechanism create or amplify?
2. **What is its ontological state?** Resource, potential, capacity, puissance, power relation, effect, or return?
3. **What dynamic is occurring?** Formation, mobilization, composition, conversion, reinvestment, capture, diversification, decay, transfer, etc.?
4. **In which domain does it occur?**

Avoid shorthand such as `money = power`, `expertise = authority`, or `brand = legitimacy`. Trace the mechanism.

## Power Show additions

A new showcase claim should state:

- the baseline limitation;
- what kOA component or mechanism changes it;
- what new puissance becomes available;
- how it composes with other capabilities;
- what power relations can emerge if the capability becomes consequential;
- what counterpowers or scope limits apply.

Do not turn aspirational capability into an unsupported deployment claim. Use the repository's maturity and claim-type labels.

## Evidence

A claim should identify at least one of:

- source IDs from [`sources/corpus-map.md`](sources/corpus-map.md);
- direct technical evidence;
- explicit analytical reconstruction;
- external research, clearly labeled.

## Branches and disagreement

Do not rewrite disagreement into artificial consensus. Preserve divergence and state the scope, evidence, trade-offs, and conditions of each claim.

## Metrics

Avoid false precision. Prefer multidimensional assessment to universal scores when constructs are not directly measurable.


## Architect-confirmed design claims

When a current design intention has been confirmed by the project architect but is not yet documented in the canonical corpus or implementation evidence, label it `USER-CONFIRMED-DESIGN` and state what technical evidence is still required.
