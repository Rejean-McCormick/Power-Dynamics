# SmartVote and Discoverability Power

SmartVote demonstrates that discovery is governance.

What rises is more likely to be reused, cited, learned, funded, adopted, or built. Even when final political equality is preserved, ranking criteria can shape the agenda before the final decision.

The case separates baseline support, credibility signals, weighted advisory readings, and discoverability effects rather than collapsing them into one score.
