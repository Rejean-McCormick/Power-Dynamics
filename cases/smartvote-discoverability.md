# SmartVote and Discoverability

This case separates decision equality from pre-decision visibility.

Even with one-person-one-vote, a system can exercise major power by ranking which proposals, resources or experts are seen. SmartVote's merit-sensitive discovery is therefore both an empowerment mechanism and a new locus of governance.

The audit asks who defines domains, weight policies, lenses and defaults; whether raw and weighted readings stay distinct; whether alternatives can propose different lenses; and whether an advisory reading can become de facto sovereign through institutional habit.
