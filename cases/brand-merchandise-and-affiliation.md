# Brand, Merchandise, and Affiliation

Brand can convert symbolic recognition into economic resources and public signaling.

A physical product can simultaneously create revenue, visible affiliation, curiosity, community recognition and wider brand exposure. A print-on-demand model increases latent scaling capacity because attention can be converted into commerce without first building heavy logistics.

The constitutional questions are ownership of the mark, authorization of "official" use, compatibility certification, dilution, and whether brand stewardship becomes a hidden sovereign over an otherwise forkable protocol.
