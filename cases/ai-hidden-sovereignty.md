# AI and Hidden Sovereignty

AI can be formally advisory while becoming practically sovereign if institutions cannot understand, challenge, reproduce, or override its outputs.

Signals include automatic adoption of model recommendations, inaccessible prompts or policies, no governed fallback, dependence on a remote provider, or model outputs treated as authoritative evidence by default.

The case tests the principle `AI assists under governance`.
