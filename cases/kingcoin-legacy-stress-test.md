# KingCoin as a Legacy Stress Test

Legacy KingCoin proposals include global governance, sanctions, blacklisting, automated penalties, and monetary enforcement.

These mechanisms create strong monetary and enforcement power and expose tensions with later commitments to non-domination, local sovereignty, branch alternatives, and effective exit.

The case is retained as an evolutionary stress test, not silently harmonized with current-core principles.
