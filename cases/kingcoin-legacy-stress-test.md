# KingCoin as a Legacy Stress Test

Earlier economic documents imagine a strong global monetary and redistribution architecture, including central governance bodies, automated penalties, sanctions and possible transaction blacklisting.

This is retained as a stress test because it conflicts sharply with later principles of non-domination, recourse, local sovereignty and effective exit.

The case is analytically valuable: a system may pursue egalitarian outcomes while constructing extremely strong `power over`. Good intentions do not remove the need to analyze enforcement authority, due process, reversibility, coercive capacity and alternatives.

The lesson is evolutionary rather than condemnatory: the corpus shows movement from "build a sufficiently just center" toward "build structures in which even a well-intentioned center cannot become terminally arbitrary."
