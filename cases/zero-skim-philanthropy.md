# Zero-Skim Philanthropy

The Zero-Skim model separates economic contribution from democratic allocation.

`Money → donation resources` does not automatically become `Money → more votes`.

A cooperative rail can provide financial and compliance puissance while members retain allocation authority through one-member-one-vote mechanisms.
