# Competing and Complementary Kristals

Two Kristals can each claim a better representation without requiring immediate centralized adjudication.

The system should preserve lineage, provenance, evidence, policy differences, scope, uncertainty, and added value.

A third branch may synthesize or specialize. The governance capability is the ability to compare and evolve rather than repeat one official answer without added value.

## Pluralism is not epistemic equivalence

Forkability protects the right to maintain incompatible representations. It does not require the system to pretend that all branches have equal support.

A branch may encode a religious cosmology, a minority scientific interpretation, a deliberately adversarial worldview, or an explicitly fictional universe. The system should preserve the branch's own axioms and lineage while also making differences in evidence, provenance, uncertainty, contradiction, and validation policy legible.

> **Freedom to fork a knowledge representation is not ownership of truth, and it is not immunity from comparison with evidence.**

A reference or institutionally stewarded Kristal should therefore function as a visible reference branch, not as an irreversible global truth monopoly.
