# Media and State: Domain-Specific Power

Media can possess enormous distribution, framing, and synchronization puissance. This can become visibility, agenda, reputation, and framing power at mass scale.

States may possess stronger legal, fiscal, coercive, territorial, or administrative authority.

The correct comparison is domain-specific rather than a one-dimensional “most powerful actor” ranking.
