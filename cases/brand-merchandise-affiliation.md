# Brand, Merchandise, and Affiliation

Brand can convert symbolic resources into economic and network returns:

`system value → brand recognition → affiliation / attention → merchandise → revenue → reinvestment`

Merchandise also creates public signaling: purchase becomes both funding and visible affiliation.

The firewall is that commercial or symbolic success should not silently become epistemic truth or institutional sovereignty.
