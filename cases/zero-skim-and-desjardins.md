# Zero-Skim Philanthropy and Desjardins

The recent philanthropy design illustrates a power conversion firewall: donors supply economic resources while allocation can remain one-member-one-vote rather than one-dollar-one-vote.

The Desjardins proposal also illustrates power stacking. A cooperative bank could simultaneously provide financial infrastructure, strong identity, certification, philanthropy and platform access. Even if each function has a legitimate rationale, their combination can create a highly consequential dependency node.

The case therefore asks whether cooperative governance is sufficient, which functions should remain separable, and what substitution paths exist if the institution's priorities drift.
