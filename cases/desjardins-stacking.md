# Desjardins and Power Stacking

The Desjardins proposal combines member governance with possible roles in banking, identity, certification, philanthropy, and Konnaxion access.

Each function may be legitimate while their combination creates cross-domain leverage. The design question is how to keep financial infrastructure strong in its mandate without silently becoming identity, epistemic, political, or platform sovereignty.
