# Changelog

## 5.0 — kOA Power Show

- Restored the repository's original front-facing purpose: **show all the major ways kOA can make individuals, groups, organizations, and federations more capable**.
- Added top-level [`POWER-SHOW.md`](POWER-SHOW.md).

- Added the **capability spectrum** and scale path from individual to federation.
- Added **composition engine**, **composition gain**, **capability surface**, **power surface**, and **capability-to-power transition** to the canonical glossary.
- Made generated collective puissance a prominent concept.
- Restored conversion chains, pre-political power, mnemonic power, counterpower, optionality, brand, network, economic, media, founder, and infrastructural capability to the visible Power Show.
- Repositioned the ontology as a precision layer rather than the repository's primary narrative.
- Reframed the constitution as **governance after capability**: the architecture exists to let kOA become powerful without making successful functions terminal sovereigns.
- Reframed branch ecology as **evolutionary capability**, not only an emergency anti-capture mechanism.
- Preserved the v4 three-axis analytical model and all major audit methods.
