# Principles

These principles are constraints for making imperfection visible, revisable, and non-terminal rather than claims that any implementation can be perfect.

## Ontological discipline

1. **A resource is not automatically power.**
2. **Potential is not capacity.**
3. **Capacity is not puissance.**
4. **Puissance is not automatically power.**
5. **Power is relational and domain-specific.**

## Capability and composition

6. **Power Dynamics studies empowerment as well as domination.**
7. **Collective puissance can be generated.**
8. **Infrastructure can amplify puissance without owning it.**
9. **Composition does not require ownership.**
10. **Different forms of puissance should remain composable without becoming automatically convertible into unrelated authority.**

## Measurement and epistemic humility

11. **No perfect human metric.**
12. **A reading is not the thing measured.** `Score ≠ person ≠ competence ≠ truth`.
13. **Support is not evidence.**
14. **Dominance is not truth.**
15. **Uncertainty and disagreement must remain representable.**

## Conversion and concentration

16. **No silent conversion.**
17. **Stacking matters.**
18. **Capture is a cross-domain conversion problem.**
19. **Instance capture must not imply system capture.**
20. **Terminal power is a primary constitutional risk.**

## Counterpower

21. **Verification + Contestation + Substitution + Exit** are foundational counterpowers.
22. **Formal exit is insufficient.**
23. **Formal forkability is insufficient.**
24. **Branch mobility matters.**
25. **Revocability and reversibility are distinct.**

## Evolutionary governance

26. **Imperfection is permanent; authority need not be.**
27. **Variation is normal.**
28. **Complementarity can be superior to convergence.**
29. **The core should protect evolution, not freeze outcomes.**
30. **Repetition without added value is not useful plurality.**

## Returns and compounding

31. **Action leaves assets behind.**
32. **Who receives the returns matters.**
33. **Returns can compound future puissance.**
34. **Compounding can also compound concentration.**

## kOA constitutional orientation

35. **Strong distributed puissance; weak sovereignty.**
36. **AI assists under governance; AI does not silently inherit institutional sovereignty.**
37. **Semantic sovereignty requires practical control, not merely formal openness.**
38. **Founder independence is a resilience criterion.**
39. **Brand stewardship and protocol governance are distinct.**
40. **The system should remain capable of learning from its own alternatives.**
