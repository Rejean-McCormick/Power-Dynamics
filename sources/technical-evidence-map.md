# Technical Evidence Map

The 28-file corpus is strong on philosophy, architecture summaries, institutional proposals, and analytical reconstructions. A rigorous next-stage audit should add direct technical specifications for the exact loci where **capacity becomes puissance and puissance becomes relational power**.

## Highest-priority evidence to import

1. **Civilizational Coherence — power/capture chapters**: capture, lock-in, verification, contestation, substitution, exit.
2. **kOA Digital Ecosystem Layer Model / Layer 0**: separation of functions and mandate boundaries.
3. **Kristal authority registry, trust roots, reader policies, federation/versioning**: epistemic puissance, authority, branch lineage.
4. **EkoH ScoreConfiguration, ScoreHistory, visibility/access policies**: who defines credibility readings and how those readings affect opportunity.
5. **SmartVote lens registry, defaults, raw/weighted storage, version governance**: decision puissance, discoverability, advisory power.
6. **Orgo RBAC, routing, escalation, closure, override rules**: operational puissance and execution power.
7. **Konnaxion moderation, discovery, identity, ranking policies**: collective puissance, attention, agenda power.
8. **Capsule Manager signing, registry, update, rollback, deployment profiles**: deployment puissance, infrastructural dependency, choke points.
9. **Koali distribution specification**: credibility portability, alternative distributions, interoperability.
10. **Brand / trademark governance and founder-control inventory**: symbolic assets, domains, repos, release keys, registries, marks, current relational control.

## Audit rule

A normative statement such as “EkoH is non-sovereign” is not sufficient technical evidence. The audit must identify the configuration, permission, key, role, registry, default, or protocol boundary that explains:

1. what resource and potential exist;
2. what becomes usable capacity;
3. what becomes effective puissance;
4. where that puissance creates relational power;
5. which counterpowers constrain that relation.

## Mobilization / mediation evidence

Also inspect release permissions, publication gates, queueing, rate limits, ranking, recommendation, translation, summarization, distribution dependencies, analytics feedback, alternative channels, moderation controls, update keys, and bottlenecks.
