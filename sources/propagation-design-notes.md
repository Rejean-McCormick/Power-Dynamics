# Propagation Design Notes — 2026-09-21

This source records current architect-confirmed mechanisms relevant to propagation and adoption that are not all fully represented in the earlier supplied source corpus.

It records **current capability claims and design intention**, not independent validation of scale or effectiveness.

## Cross-branch returns

Current intention:

> Successful branches should be able to return money, credibility, evidence, code, methods, relationships, operators, or other reusable assets to shared infrastructure and other branches.

A branch may therefore finance or de-risk another branch without requiring all branches to share the same business model.

## Tools that build tools

Current reported practice:

> AI-assisted development methods and internal development / diagnostic / deployment tooling are used to increase the speed and quality with which additional kOA tooling can be built.

Evidence needed for external claims:

- comparable effort measurements;
- repeatability by non-founder operators;
- regression / quality metrics;
- documented automation reuse across repositories.

## Worlds

Current reported capability:

> Konnaxion can be seeded with structured simulated or reconstructed Worlds derived from public sources, allowing a demonstration environment to begin with realistic actors, questions, positions, sources, and relationships rather than an empty platform.

Required epistemic distinction:

```text
source material
≠ reconstruction
≠ simulation
≠ live contribution
```

A World demonstrates representational and demonstration capacity. It does not by itself prove adoption or real-world impact.

## Cinematic demonstration pipeline

Current reported capability:

> A cinematic demonstration engine, combined with interface tooling and browser automation, can generate repeatable high-quality product demonstrations from scripted tours.

Propagation hypothesis:

> Lowering the marginal cost of contextual demonstrations can lower the explanation barrier for new audiences.

Evidence needed:

- production time per demonstration;
- viewer → evaluator conversion;
- evaluator → pilot conversion;
- repeatability across languages / countries / domains.

## Machine-readable documentation

Current reported practice / intention:

> Documentation is increasingly optimized for AI-assisted reading through structured Markdown, indexes, context packs, and `llms.txt`-style concise machine entry surfaces.

Propagation hypothesis:

> If external reviewers can use their own AI systems to navigate the corpus, due-diligence and explanation costs may fall.

## Multilingual scale

Current reported milestone:

> A complete Albanian implementation has been produced in a Grammatical Framework-oriented language architecture, strengthening the plausibility of systematic expansion toward much broader language coverage.

The 300-language target remains a scale objective, not a claim of current equal-quality support.

Recommended evidence model:

- language conformance tiers;
- compile / grammar tests;
- gold corpus metrics;
- native-speaker review;
- human hours and AI-assisted hours;
- coverage across language families and scripts.

## Cultural and narrative diffusion

Current assets include multiple books, albums, podcasts, web material, essays, and narrative surfaces intended to explain complex architecture through different cognitive entry modes.

These assets should remain optional to operational adoption.

`Narrative usefulness ≠ factual authority.`

## Pre-exposure / outreach

Current strategy includes earlier outreach to people and institutions in several sectors before later deployment phases.

Epistemic rule:

`contacted ≠ received ≠ read ≠ familiar ≠ supportive ≠ partner`

The only defensible propagation claim without stronger evidence is that prior contact can create a possibility of later recognition.

## Founder exposure and pseudonymity

Current practice uses a public architect identity and a separate narrative persona / pseudonym.

The persona can provide creative and exposure optionality.

It must not be treated as the reason the system is resilient.

System resilience depends on independent reproducibility, distributed mastery, public artifacts, explicit authority, branchability, and continuity during founder absence.

## Movement self-use

Current design intention:

> When multiple independent participants exist, the kOA initiative can use kOA tooling to coordinate parts of its own work, allowing the movement / community to become a real operational test environment.

The relevant threshold is not a branding declaration that a “movement” exists. It is evidence of multi-actor independent capability.
