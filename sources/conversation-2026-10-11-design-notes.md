# Architect discussion: Gather to Liberate and polycentric trust

**Status:** design intentions confirmed directly by Réjean McCormick in the October 2026 conversation; **not** a record of implemented functionality, institutional agreements, or measured social effects. This file is a reconstruction, not a verbatim transcript. S31 refers to that conversation.

## Confirmed intentions

1. **UCKK concerns understanding, not merely disbelief.** The aim is to build the ability to investigate narratives, inspect evidence, understand systems, and reach revisable judgments. Replacing an official narrative with a counter-narrative is not sufficient.
2. **kOA concerns empowerment through a sociotechnical operating system.** Understanding should become the practical capacity to coordinate, decide, act, and remember.
3. **Gather to Liberate counters Divide and Conquer.** Joining forces should expand participants' agency without requiring agreement on a single doctrine or submission to a single operator.
4. **Open Good / forkable instances.** The intended ecosystem consists of independently reproducible, interoperable deployments without dependence on a central proprietary platform. The architect explicitly wants participants to retain an alternative even if the founder becomes corrupt or unreliable.
5. **Power centers are allowed.** Universities, cities, clubs, public bodies, companies, laboratories, and international institutions can be authoritative within bounded contexts. Their coexistence is not an architectural failure.
6. **Recognition is reciprocal, scoped and selective.** Any institution may choose to recognize all, some, or none of another institution's attestations. Recognition must not be forced by upstream hierarchy or automatically become unlimited transitive trust.
7. **Recognition policies may automate acceptance.** Examples discussed include an institution accepting Harvard-issued qualifications, health-related attestations from an appropriate health body, or Microsoft-authored documentation for Microsoft products, subject to declared domains and limits. These are *hypothetical policy examples*, not claims about current UNESCO, Harvard, WHO, or Microsoft agreements.
8. **Institutional legitimacy is contestable.** If an authority loses support through misconduct or poor performance, alternative institutions can form and earn recognition. A clone of a *software/protocol implementation* is not the same as legally cloning an institution's name, mandate, trademark, or public-law authority.
9. **Information comparison is a priority use case.** Conflicting news narratives, selective coverage, misleading success stories, and inconsistent fact-checks should become inspectable through claim-level evidence, provenance, omission indicators, and multiple independent trust policies. Agreement counts must not determine truth.

## Analytical inferences — proposed, not architect-confirmed implementation

- Trust needs a typed and direction-specific **recognition edge**, not merely a single global trust score.
- The network needs **non-transitivity by default**, explicit delegation limits, claim-specific scope, policy versions, revocation, provenance, and effective portability.
- A fork should be an **institutional exit path**, not a magical guarantee of portability or legitimacy; data, identities, relationships, interoperability contracts, licensing, and practical switching costs still matter.
- **Information weaponization** spans true-but-unrepresentative examples, survivorship bias, framing, agenda selection, repetition, and evidence laundering—not just false statements.
- The architecture should distinguish **epistemic validity**, **authenticity of issuer**, **institutional trust**, **public recognition**, **legal authority**, and **social popularity**.

## Non-claims

- No UNESCO partnership, endorsement, deployment, certification service, or replacement is asserted.
- No empirical claim is made that contemporary media manipulation or institutional corruption has been reduced by kOA.
- Neither the mythological analogy to Quetzalcóatl nor the King Klown persona establishes historical continuity or superior authority.
- The proposal is compatible with disagreement, editorial choice, and minority scientific positions; disagreement alone is not evidence of disinformation.
