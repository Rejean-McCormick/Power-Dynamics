# Architect-Confirmed Design Notes

## Koali ownership and distribution

Current intention:

> Koali should not remain the exclusive personal property or terminal credibility authority of its founder. Its useful capability should be duplicable, shareable, branchable, and capable of plural distributions around interoperable structures.
