# SemantiK Architect and SenTient

SemantiK Architect concerns the architecture of meaning: concepts, categories, relations, contexts, and multilingual mappings. SenTient concerns reconciliation of ambiguous or uncertain propositions, entity relations, and semantic interpretation.

These functions create semantic puissance but can also create **constitutive power** if their categories become mandatory or opaque.

Semantic sovereignty therefore requires versioned ontologies, explicit uncertainty, local control where appropriate, traceable transformations, and meaningful alternatives.
