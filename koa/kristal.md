# Kristal

Kristal creates **epistemic and mnemonic puissance** by structuring claims, provenance, status, versioning, lineage, validation, and reusable artifacts.

Potential power relations arise around authority registries, trust roots, validation thresholds, canonical/reference promotion, reader policies, release/version selection, and federation/import rules.

Plurality is constitutionally important: competing or complementary Kristals can preserve different evidence, interpretations, scopes, policies, or even incompatible worldview axioms. The system should make lineage and differences legible rather than silently declaring one permanent universal truth.

This includes three distinct branch types that should not be conflated:

- **reference branches** — maintained as broadly useful encyclopedic or institutional references;
- **worldview branches** — intentionally adapt assumptions or interpretations to a community's beliefs;
- **fictional / experimental branches** — alter world axioms for narrative, simulation, or adversarial testing.

Forkability does not imply equal evidentiary support. A system can permit a branch to exist while still making provenance, contradiction, uncertainty, validation policy, and source independence visible.

The strongest technical audit still requires direct current specifications for trust roots, policies, registries, signatures, and federation behavior.
