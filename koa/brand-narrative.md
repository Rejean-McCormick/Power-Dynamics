# Brand, Narrative, and Affiliation

Brand and narrative create communicative, symbolic, and affiliation puissance. They can compress complex meaning into recognizable symbols, lower transmission cost, mobilize supporters, generate commerce, and make a system culturally legible.

`system value → brand recognition → attention / affiliation → revenue / network → reinvestment`

The constitutional firewall is that brand or narrative does not become truth, expertise, political legitimacy, or protocol sovereignty by itself.


## Narrative self-binding

The corpus also uses fiction to publish explicit ethical constraints: clarity of intention, vigilance against monopoly and domination, recognition of contributors, self-mockery, revision, and non-dogmatic transmission.

This creates a **narrative self-binding** mechanism. The symbolic layer does not only transmit affiliation; it also transmits limits that participants and critics can reuse against symbolic capture.

Public ethics do not replace enforceable governance, but they redirect some narrative power away from unquestionable persona authority and toward explicit principles.

See [`../cases/ethical-fiction-self-binding.md`](../cases/ethical-fiction-self-binding.md).

## Sharing symbolic capital

The narrative ethics do more than limit abuse. They also redirect symbolic returns: recognize contributors, thank allies publicly, share merit, practice self-mockery, reject dogma, document, transmit, and pass the baton.

That means the narrative layer can be designed to **share symbolic capital rather than merely accumulate followers around a persona**. The public-brand distinction between mobilization persona and architect identity reinforces the same direction: the persona functions as a diffusion instrument, not the source of protocol validity.
