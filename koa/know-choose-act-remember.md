# Know → Choose → Act → Remember

The kOA loop is a capability-regeneration cycle.

### Know
Transform dispersed contributions into structured, provenance-preserving knowledge without prematurely erasing ambiguity.

### Choose
Transform knowledge into legible orientation while preserving reasons, options, disagreement, and multiple readings.

### Act
Transform orientation into responsibilities, tasks, routing, validation, escalation, and outcomes.

### Remember
Transform experience into reusable memory that lowers the cost of future action.

The political value lies in the **transitions**, because each transition can become a bottleneck or locus of power.
