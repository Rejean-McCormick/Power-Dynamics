# Konnaxion: Association, Attention, and Public Coordination

Konnaxion increases `power with` by helping people, knowledge, projects and expertise find one another. It also creates attention and agenda power because discovery, ranking, moderation and default visibility determine what becomes actionable.

A federated architecture can reduce platform-owner sovereignty, but federation alone does not solve attention concentration. Real analysis requires routing policies, moderation authority, identity dependencies, default feeds and the practical ability of alternative hubs to become visible.

The constitutional objective is a strong coordination surface without a sovereign network owner.
