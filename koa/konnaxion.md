# Konnaxion

Konnaxion creates **associative, communicative, discovery, and coordination puissance** by making people, projects, knowledge, deliberation, and actions visible and connectable.

Power relations may arise through moderation, ranking, recommendation, search, routing, identity, access controls, default feeds, and institutional recognition.

Because discoverability is governance, Konnaxion should be analyzed as a pre-political mediation infrastructure, not merely a social interface.
