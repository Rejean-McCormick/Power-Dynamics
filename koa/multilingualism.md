# Multilingualism, Reach, and Semantic Participation

Multilingualism creates two distinct capacities.

**Reach puissance** expands geographic and linguistic participation.

**Semantic participation puissance** lets communities participate without abandoning their language, categories, and local meaning structures.

Translation is therefore political. A system can claim global reach while imposing one dominant ontology through translation defaults. Traceability, uncertainty, local revision, and plural mappings are important safeguards.
