# AI and Cognitive Power

AI can greatly increase cognitive and development capacity: synthesis, translation, extraction, coding, testing, pattern detection and scenario generation.

The architecture's political requirement is separation between **intelligence and sovereignty**. Probabilistic systems may accelerate candidate generation, but binding institutional transformations should remain attributable to explicit rules, validated artifacts and accountable authority.

Power Dynamics also treats access to AI as capital. During bootstrap, AI can compress development cost and convert limited human time into a much larger technical portfolio. That gain should be analyzed without pretending the AI has independent institutional legitimacy.
