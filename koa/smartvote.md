# SmartVote

SmartVote creates **comparative judgment and discoverability puissance** by preserving multiple readings of support rather than hiding evaluation inside one opaque score.

A critical separation is between baseline/raw democratic readings and expertise/ethics or other advisory readings.

The political baseline is not silently superseded by advisory weight. Domain-bounded credibility informs rather than automatically governs.

Power relations arise through lens selection, defaults, criteria, discoverability, ranking, and institutional interpretation of weighted results. Those elements require explicit registries, versioning, auditability, and branch alternatives.
## Academic constraints

The external-research layer documents several constraints directly relevant to SmartVote:

- expert performance is strongly domain- and task-bounded;
- nonexperts need evidence for deciding which experts to trust;
- calibrated / contribution-sensitive weighting can improve some aggregates;
- diversity can add problem-solving value that is lost by selecting only individually high-scoring participants;
- social-choice systems face manipulation and impossibility constraints under broad conditions;
- any reputation or weighting metric becomes a gaming surface once it affects opportunity or authority.

See [`../Academic-Lineage/06-expertise-smartvote-and-collective-judgment.md`](../Academic-Lineage/06-expertise-smartvote-and-collective-judgment.md) and [`../Academic-Lineage/07-metrics-reputation-and-gaming.md`](../Academic-Lineage/07-metrics-reputation-and-gaming.md).

These references constrain the design; they do not validate a particular SmartVote formula.
