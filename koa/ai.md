# AI and Cognitive Puissance

AI can greatly increase cognitive throughput: reading, synthesis, comparison, generation, translation, semantic processing, and planning.

The repository treats AI as **cognitive puissance under governance**, not as an autonomous institutional sovereign.

Risks include hidden policy through prompts/defaults, opacity through complexity, semantic centralization, remote-service dependence, unreviewable automation, and substitution of model confidence for governed evidence.

AI should assist transformations whose authority remains externally governed and auditable.
