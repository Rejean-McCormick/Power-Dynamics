# Capsules and Federation

Capsules and federation create **infrastructural autonomy puissance**: local deployment, signed packages, verification, rollback, self-hosting, offline operation, and independent continuity.

Their political importance is counterpower. A system is less terminal when communities can continue operating, substitute a deployment, verify artifacts, or form branches without permanent permission from one remote operator.

The technical power audit should inspect signing keys, registries, update channels, compatibility contracts, and recovery procedures.
