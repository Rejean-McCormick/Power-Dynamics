# Federation, Capsules, and Branch Mobility

Self-hosting, portable artifacts, offline operation and deployable capsules can reduce dependency on a single cloud operator. Their political value is the ability to preserve local continuity and support alternative branches.

However, hidden centrality may remain in signing keys, update channels, registries, domains, compatibility rules or trademark. A technically independent instance is not fully sovereign if it cannot receive trusted updates, preserve identity or remain interoperable without permission from one center.

The correct test is therefore effective branch mobility, not merely the existence of source code.
