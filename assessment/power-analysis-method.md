# Power Analysis Method

For each system, relation or branch, answer:

1. What incapacity or dependency exists before the mechanism?
2. What resource enters?
3. What capability is created?
4. Who holds it?
5. Who benefits?
6. Whose `power over` is reduced or increased?
7. Through which mechanism is power exercised?
8. What returns are produced?
9. Who receives the returns?
10. Where can returns be reinvested?
11. Which cross-domain conversions are possible?
12. Which conversions should be firewalled?
13. Where does power stack?
14. Which dependencies result?
15. Can affected actors verify, contest, substitute or exit?
16. Can an alternative branch become viable?
17. What remains uncertain or unmeasurable?
