# Power Analysis Method

Use this sequence for any actor, institution, subsystem, or branch.

1. **Problem / baseline** — what incapacity, dependency, or coordination problem exists?
2. **Resources** — what stocks and assets are available?
3. **Potential** — what possibilities do they create?
4. **Capacity** — what is organized enough to be used?
5. **Puissance** — what can actually be mobilized now?
6. **Mobilization** — reservoir, release, throughput, channels, mediation, amplification.
7. **Power relations** — whose possibilities change, and how?
8. **Returns** — what assets are created and who receives them?
9. **Conversion pathways** — what can become what?
10. **Stacking / concentration** — what accumulates in one node?
11. **Dependency** — what becomes hard to substitute or exit?
12. **Counterpowers** — verification, contestation, substitution, exit, fork, branch mobility.
13. **Temporal analysis** — compounding, decay, succession, lock-in.
14. **Constitutional requirements** — what should be scoped, separated, portable, or reversible?
