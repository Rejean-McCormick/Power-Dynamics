# Power Balance Sheet

A power balance sheet maps accumulated assets and dependencies without pretending they have one common unit.

### Assets / stocks
financial, technical, epistemic, human, network, credibility, brand, institutional, operational, mnemonic, optionality.

### Dependencies / liabilities
single providers, single maintainers, remote services, legal permissions, proprietary formats, recognition monopolies, key-person knowledge, network effects, update/signing keys, funding concentration.

The purpose is to expose conversion pathways and terminal dependencies, not to produce a net “power score.”
