# Power Balance Sheet

A power balance sheet inventories both assets and dependencies.

## Assets

Financial resources, time, knowledge, code, infrastructure, relationships, credibility, brand, institutional access, memory, legal rights, data and optionality.

## Dependencies / liabilities

Cloud providers, one maintainer, one signing key, one domain, one funder, one reputation system, one identity provider, proprietary formats, one critical institution or a high migration cost.

## Flows

Record which assets are consumed, which capabilities are produced, what returns appear and where they are reinvested.

The balance sheet is not a monetary valuation. It is a structured picture of what an actor can mobilize and what could interrupt that capability.
