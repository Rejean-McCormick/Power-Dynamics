# Dependency and Exit Audit

Assess effective dependency through alternatives, migration time, data portability, identity portability, reputation portability, legal barriers, network loss, retraining cost, infrastructure requirements, institutional recognition, recovery, and rollback.

Report both **formal exit** and **effective exit**. Their difference is often the locus of hidden power.
