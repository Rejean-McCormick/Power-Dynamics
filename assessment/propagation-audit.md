# Propagation Audit

Use this audit to determine whether a claimed growth mechanism actually propagates capability or merely creates attention, dependency, or local compounding.

## A. Loop identity

1. What is the loop called?
2. What actor / branch / domain owns each stage?
3. What begins the loop?
4. What event counts as closure?

## B. Return

5. What reusable return is produced?
6. Is it rival or non-rival?
7. Is it portable?
8. Does it retain provenance?
9. Who receives it?

## C. Propagation

10. Which other actor / branch can consume the return?
11. What activation barrier does it lower?
12. How much does it lower that barrier?
13. Can the transfer occur without founder intervention?
14. Can the transfer occur without mandatory central service dependency?

## D. Coupling

15. Which other loop does this feed?
16. Does that loop return an asset back?
17. Is the coupling intentional or accidental?
18. What shared dependency could break both loops?

## E. Governance

19. What authority grows as the loop grows?
20. What cross-domain conversion becomes possible?
21. What prevents money, credibility, brand, expertise, or infrastructure from becoming unrelated sovereignty?
22. What exit / substitution / fork remains practical?

## F. Damping

23. What causes saturation?
24. What slows or stops the loop when quality declines?
25. What signal distinguishes healthy growth from runaway growth?
26. Can operators intentionally pause or remain local?

## G. Evidence

27. Is the loop only designed, demonstrated, used, repeated, independently replicated, or federated?
28. What metric is currently observed?
29. What metric is only projected?
30. What result would falsify the mechanism?

## H. Founder-dependency test

31. Founder minutes per cycle?
32. Founder-only credentials / keys / relationships?
33. Founder-only interpretive knowledge?
34. Can another operator close the loop?
35. Can another operator teach a third operator?

A claimed propagation mechanism should not be considered mature until at least one non-founder actor has closed the relevant loop independently.
