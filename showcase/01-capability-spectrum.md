# The kOA Capability Spectrum

The Power Show begins by separating **what kOA helps actors do** from **what authority any actor has over others**.

The first category is **puissance**: effectively mobilizable capacity.

## Epistemic puissance

Create, structure, compare, verify, preserve, cite, derive, and reuse knowledge. Kristal is the primary architectural locus.

## Semantic puissance

Make categories, meanings, relations, ambiguity, translation, and ontology explicit enough to inspect and evolve. SemantiK / SenTient are central here.

## Cognitive puissance

Reason, synthesize, model, plan, generate, evaluate, and learn at greater throughput. AI can amplify this capability, but its outputs remain subordinate to explicit governance and provenance.

## Individual agency

Understand options, make informed choices, refuse, exit, learn, publish, organize, and preserve personal or institutional optionality.

## Collective puissance

Combine people who individually possess only partial knowledge, time, skills, resources, and relationships into coordinated capability.

## Network puissance

Find expertise, collaborators, communities, institutions, supporters, customers, contributors, or alternative branches. Network capability changes what can be attempted.

## Discoverability puissance

Make relevant knowledge, people, needs, proposals, branches, and evidence findable. Discoverability is empowering before it becomes a ranking or agenda relation.

## Credibility puissance

Make evidence of contribution, expertise, reliability, or domain-bounded competence legible enough to be used. `Score ≠ competence`; credibility signals are governed readings, not persons.

## Deliberative and decision puissance

Compare alternatives, preserve baseline equality, add advisory expertise without silently replacing the baseline, and make reasons inspectable.

## Coordination puissance

Sequence actors and information so that many partial capabilities produce coherent action.

## Operational puissance

Route work, create ownership, set escalation paths, manage time windows, review outcomes, and close loops.

## Mnemonic puissance

Preserve reasons, provenance, decisions, actions, failures, outcomes, versions, and lineage so future action begins with memory rather than amnesia.

## Economic and allocation puissance

Mobilize, pool, direct, or govern resources. Economic contribution can support collective action without automatically purchasing political authority.

## Technical puissance

Build, inspect, modify, test, reproduce, repair, deploy, integrate, and maintain systems.

## Infrastructural puissance

Create shared capability that lowers the cost of future action for many actors: protocols, schemas, identity contracts, deployment mechanisms, knowledge formats, workflow primitives.

## Distribution puissance

Move knowledge and coordination across languages, communities, institutions, and geographic boundaries.

## Brand and affiliation puissance

Create recognition, public signaling, identity, community affiliation, and transmission. Brand can create attention and funding without becoming truth or sovereignty.

## Federation puissance

Operate local instances while preserving interoperability, lineage, portability, and the possibility of alternative governance.

## Exit and substitution puissance

Leave, replace a dependency, preserve assets, self-host, migrate, or continue through another implementation.

## Forking puissance

Create an alternative implementation, policy, ontology, credibility model, reader policy, governance model, or knowledge representation.

## Evolutionary puissance

Do more than fork: demonstrate improvement, gather support, remain interoperable, recombine useful ideas, and allow dominant branches to change or be replaced.

## The key distinction

A capability is not automatically a relation of power.

> **The Power Show maps what actors become able to do. Power Dynamics then asks whose possibilities change because they can do it.**
