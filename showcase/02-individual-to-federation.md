# From Individual to Federation

kOA's power show is also a **scale show**. The same architecture can increase capability at different social scales without assuming that every scale requires the same center.

## Individual

Potential gains include:

- better access to structured knowledge;
- stronger personal memory and provenance;
- AI-assisted cognition;
- better discovery of expertise and opportunities;
- easier publication and reuse;
- more informed choice;
- greater exit and optionality.

## Group

A group can gain capabilities no member has alone:

- shared context;
- distributed expertise;
- collective comparison;
- task routing;
- explicit ownership;
- coordination;
- reusable memory;
- stronger network reach.

This is **generated collective puissance**.

## Organization

At organizational scale, the architecture adds:

- roles and mandates;
- governed workflows;
- escalation;
- decision records;
- institutional memory;
- auditability;
- repeatable operating patterns;
- succession and continuity.

## Federation

A federation can add:

- local sovereignty over local operation;
- shared interoperability contracts;
- portable knowledge;
- distributed deployment;
- branchable governance;
- independent continuity;
- cross-instance learning;
- substitution of providers or implementations.

The target architecture is:

> **StrongHub + DistributedCapability + ReplicableWhole**

This permits useful centrality without making the center the sole source of truth, continuity, identity, or capability.

## Scaling without scaling sovereignty

The important design challenge is:

> **Can capability scale faster than dependency on any single sovereign node?**

That is why portability, federation, explicit authority scope, provenance, branch mobility, and local operation belong inside the power show rather than being afterthoughts.
