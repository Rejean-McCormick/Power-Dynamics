# kOA Component Power Matrix

Every kOA component should be read in three steps:

1. **What resource does it organize?**
2. **What puissance does it create?**
3. **What power relations can emerge around successful use?**

| Component | Generated / amplified puissance | Possible power relations | Power-routing / sharing mechanisms | Constitutional counterweights |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SemantiK / SenTient | semantic structuration, ambiguity resolution, translation | classification, ontology, admissibility, framing | explicit ontologies and local semantic governance make meaning inspectable and reproducible | provenance, contestable categories, alternatives |
| Kristal | epistemic + mnemonic puissance | canonization, validation, trust-root, standard-setting power | portable artifacts, lineage, derivation, competing Kristals distribute epistemic capacity | provenance, reader policies, trust-root plurality, migration |
| Konnaxion | collective, network, communication, discovery puissance | ranking, moderation, access, visibility, agenda power | creator/community spaces, portability, federation, alternative discovery paths | transparent policies, export, alternative discovery layers |
| Koali / EkoH | credibility legibility | recognition, standing, certification, opportunity power | duplicable / shareable Koali distributions; domain-bounded policies; portable evidence | history, plurality of models, contestation, interoperability |
| SmartVote | comparative and deliberative puissance | lens, default, weighting, discoverability influence | expertise routed into advisory readings rather than automatic sovereignty | preserve baseline, deterministic calculation, explicit lenses |
| Orgo | operational and administrative puissance | routing, assignment, escalation, timing, closure power | explicit roles and ownership distribute operational agency instead of leaving it implicit | audit trails, appeal, workflow rules, revocability |
| Capsules / federation | deployment, replication, substitution, local-operation puissance | signing, update, compatibility, registry power | central infrastructure converted into local self-hosting and independent continuity | verifiable packages, rollback, multiple hosts, branchability, key governance |
| AI | cognitive throughput and synthesis puissance | hidden framing, prioritization, interpretation, dependency | cognitive amplification delivered under explicit policy rather than model sovereignty | provenance, bounded roles, human-governed authority, reproducible paths |
| Brand / narrative | recognition, affiliation, distribution, fundraising puissance | attention, legitimacy-by-association, trademark gatekeeping | ethical principles embedded in fiction; attribution; architecture-first narrative; protocol/brand separation | brand ≠ truth; public self-binding; criticism; separate stewardship |
| Multilingual layer | semantic access and distribution puissance | translation framing, language inclusion/exclusion | local semantic control and multilingual access redirect linguistic power toward communities | explicit source links, review, non-equivalence notes |

## The design goal

> **Increase generated puissance aggressively, route it deliberately, make resulting power relations legible, and keep counterpower effective.**

This is the shortest summary of the repo's normative architecture.
