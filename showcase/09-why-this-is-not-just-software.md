# Why kOA Is Not Just Software

Software is one layer of kOA's capability architecture.

The broader system combines:

- knowledge structures;
- semantic conventions;
- provenance;
- credibility interpretations;
- coordination rules;
- decision procedures;
- workflows;
- institutional memory;
- identity and affiliation;
- deployment infrastructure;
- governance;
- branching and federation;
- human and AI cognition.

That means kOA can affect not only computation but **what a collective can know, notice, compare, decide, coordinate, preserve, and reproduce**.

## Capability infrastructure

A useful definition is:

> **kOA is capability infrastructure for converting knowledge into governed collective action and action back into reusable knowledge.**

Its deeper loop is:

```text
knowledge
→ orientation
→ choice
→ execution
→ experience
→ memory
→ better knowledge
```

## Why infrastructure matters politically

Infrastructure disappears into normality when it works. Once widely adopted, technical defaults and interfaces can become de facto political boundaries.

A powerful infrastructure may therefore create:

- standard-setting power;
- compatibility power;
- identity gatekeeping;
- discoverability power;
- administrative power;
- mnemonic power;
- dependency and lock-in.

The objective is not to avoid infrastructure. It is to make infrastructural puissance **governable, portable, inspectable, and non-terminal**.
