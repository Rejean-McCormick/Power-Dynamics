# Political, Economic, and Monetary Power

These domains interact strongly but should not be collapsed.

## Political power

Political power concerns who can shape:

- political standing;
- agenda;
- deliberation;
- decision rights;
- representation;
- mandates;
- amendment;
- vetoes;
- legitimacy;
- allocation decisions;
- execution of public decisions.

kOA touches political power across the full chain:

> **Meaning → Knowledge → Visibility → Credibility → Agenda → Deliberation → Decision → Execution → Memory**

This means a system can preserve formal equality at the ballot and still create major political asymmetries upstream.

### kOA mechanisms

- SemantiK / Kristal: what becomes intelligible and evidentially stable;
- Konnaxion: what becomes discoverable and discussable;
- Koali / EkoH: which competence or contribution becomes legible;
- SmartVote: how alternative readings of a decision become visible;
- Orgo: how a decision becomes implementation;
- memory: what becomes precedent;
- branch ecology: how institutional alternatives emerge.

## Economic power

Economic power concerns control or mobilization of scarce resources:

- money;
- property;
- productive assets;
- labor and time;
- infrastructure;
- funding;
- procurement;
- contracts;
- philanthropy;
- access to markets.

Money is a resource stock. When mobilizable, it becomes economic puissance. When used to alter another actor's options, bargaining position, access, or allocation, it becomes relational economic power.

```text
money
→ purchasing / funding / bargaining puissance
→ access / allocation / dependency effects
```

### kOA mechanisms

- Zero-Skim: common resource pooling and democratic allocation;
- brand / merchandise: symbolic → economic conversion;
- credibility: opportunity and funding access;
- networks: collaborators, customers, institutions, opportunities;
- execution: turning funding into actual capability;
- compounding: returns become future assets.

## Monetary power

Monetary power concerns the infrastructure and rules of exchange itself:

- issuance;
- unit of account;
- liquidity;
- settlement;
- payment rails;
- reserves;
- convertibility;
- credit and debt conditions;
- access to transaction systems.

This domain can create exceptional leverage because nearly every economic actor depends on exchange infrastructure.

### Legacy KingCoin as stress test

The older KingCoin material proposes highly centralized functions including monetary coordination, taxation, redistribution, sanctions, settlement, and public investment. In the current repository these are **LEGACY-PROPOSAL** material.

Their analytical value is to show the danger of:

```text
monetary authority
+ taxation
+ allocation
+ sanctions
+ governance
+ transaction infrastructure
= extreme power stacking
```

The evolution of Power Dynamics moves away from “design a sufficiently just center” toward:

> **Design powerful functions that do not become terminal arbitrary authority.**

## Conversion firewalls between the three domains

| Conversion | Status |
|---|---|
| money → funding | desirable |
| funding → infrastructure | desirable |
| infrastructure → broader capability | desirable |
| money → proportional political standing | blocked by principle |
| credibility → informed advice | desirable |
| credibility → sovereign decision right | blocked by principle |
| monetary infrastructure → arbitrary exclusion | constitutional risk |
| administrative execution → mandate redefinition | blocked by principle |

## The key question

> **How can material and monetary capability increase collective agency without converting wealth, rails, or administration into unaccountable political sovereignty?**

That is a core Power Dynamics question, not a side topic.
