# kOA Power Show

This directory is the **front stage** of Power Dynamics.

It answers one question first:

> **What kinds of effective capability can kOA create, compose, preserve, distribute, and evolve?**

Only after that does it ask what relations of power emerge around successful capability and how those relations should be governed.

## Guided sequence

1. [`01-capability-spectrum.md`](01-capability-spectrum.md) — everything kOA can make easier or more effective.
2. [`02-individual-to-federation.md`](02-individual-to-federation.md) — capability across scale.
3. [`03-composition-engine.md`](03-composition-engine.md) — why the whole can exceed the sum of components.
4. [`04-compounding-engine.md`](04-compounding-engine.md) — how action leaves assets that increase later capability.
5. [`05-conversion-chains.md`](05-conversion-chains.md) — how knowledge, credibility, network, money, brand, and infrastructure convert into one another.
6. [`06-pre-political-chain.md`](06-pre-political-chain.md) — meaning, visibility, credibility, agenda, decision, execution, memory.
7. [`07-component-power-matrix.md`](07-component-power-matrix.md) — each kOA subsystem: generated puissance, resulting power relations, counterpowers.
8. [`08-governance-after-capability.md`](08-governance-after-capability.md) — why the project needs a constitution precisely because it can become powerful.
9. [`09-why-this-is-not-just-software.md`](09-why-this-is-not-just-software.md) — kOA as capability infrastructure.
10. [`10-counterpower-and-optionality.md`](10-counterpower-and-optionality.md) — verification, contestation, substitution, exit, forkability, and strategic optionality as positive capability.

The analytical machinery that supports these pages lives in [`../framework/`](../framework/), [`../dynamics/`](../dynamics/), [`../puissance/`](../puissance/), [`../relations/`](../relations/), and [`../constitution/`](../constitution/).

11. [`11-power-routing-and-sharing.md`](11-power-routing-and-sharing.md) — where the power created by kOA is deliberately redirected, shared, devolved, or self-bound.
