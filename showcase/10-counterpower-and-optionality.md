# Counterpower and Optionality as Capability

The Power Show includes not only the ability to **do more**, but also the ability to **depend less**.

A system that can coordinate, rank, certify, route, or preserve memory is powerful. A participant who can verify, contest, substitute, exit, fork, or wait also possesses important capability.

## Verification puissance

Ability to independently inspect claims, provenance, signatures, rules, calculations, policies, and technical behavior.

## Contestation puissance

Ability to challenge a decision, score, category, workflow, authority, branch, or interpretation through a process capable of producing change.

## Substitution puissance

Ability to replace a provider, implementation, trust root, reader policy, workflow, branch, or institution with a viable alternative.

## Exit puissance

Ability to leave while preserving enough identity, history, knowledge, reputation, data, rights, relationships, and continuity for departure to be meaningful.

## Forking puissance

Ability to create an alternative rather than accept the existing implementation as terminal.

## Branch mobility

Ability to move beyond a symbolic fork: attract maintainers, preserve interoperability, gain discoverability, gather support, and become institutionally viable.

## Optionality puissance

Ability to preserve future choices rather than commit them prematurely.

Examples include:

- retained financial reserves;
- unpublished knowledge;
- unused deployment capacity;
- privacy and unrevealed identity;
- multiple providers;
- portable credentials;
- branchable software;
- independent infrastructure.

Optionality is important because **unexercised capability can still change future strategic position**.

## The inversion

Traditional power analysis often asks who can compel whom.

Power Dynamics also asks:

> **Who can refuse? Who can leave? Who can reproduce? Who can create an alternative? Who can preserve the option to act later?**

These are central forms of practical freedom and therefore belong inside the Power Show itself.
