# The Pre-Political Chain

Power begins before formal voting or command.

> **Meaning → Knowledge → Visibility → Credibility → Agenda → Deliberation → Decision → Execution → Memory**

kOA touches nearly every step.

## Meaning

Categories determine what can be described, compared, included, or excluded. Semantic infrastructure can create semantic puissance; control over definitions can create semantic power.

## Knowledge

What becomes structured, provenance-bearing, canonical, disputed, or reusable affects later reasoning.

## Visibility

What is discoverable has a higher chance of being cited, funded, reused, adopted, or debated. Discoverability is therefore both capability and governance.

## Credibility

Signals about expertise or contribution can help groups navigate complexity. They can also affect opportunity and standing.

## Agenda

What is surfaced repeatedly becomes more likely to be treated as worthy of collective attention.

## Deliberation and decision

Decision tools can expand comparative capacity. Defaults, lenses, weighting, and presentation can also shape the decision environment.

## Execution

Routing, ownership, time, escalation, and closure determine whether a nominal decision becomes an effective outcome.

## Memory

What is preserved becomes precedent, evidence, training data, institutional learning, and the starting point for future decisions.

## Why this belongs in the Power Show

The impressive capability of kOA is precisely that it links these stages. The governance challenge is that a system spanning these stages could also accumulate pre-political leverage if the same node controls too many transitions.
