# Systems, Cybernetics, Feedback, and Adaptation

Power Dynamics is not only a theory of authority. It is also a theory of **capability formation, feedback, memory, and evolutionary adaptation**.

## W. Ross Ashby — requisite variety

Ashby's cybernetics is associated with the principle that effective regulation requires sufficient variety in the regulator to respond to the variety of disturbances in the system.

**Correspondence:** centralized simplification can fail when local contexts vary more than the center can represent. Federation, local autonomy, plural branches, and specialized modules can preserve response variety.

Reference: W. Ross Ashby, *An Introduction to Cybernetics*, 1956.

## Stafford Beer — viable systems and recursive autonomy

Beer's Viable System Model studies how viable organizations combine operational autonomy with coordination, control, intelligence, and identity across recursive levels.

**Correspondence:** kOA's local / federated architecture and the attempt to avoid both total centralization and incoherent fragmentation.

Reference: Stafford Beer, *Brain of the Firm*, 1972; later editions.

## Donella Meadows — leverage points

Meadows' systems work distinguishes interventions at different depths, from parameters to information flows, rules, system goals, and paradigms.

**Correspondence:** Power Dynamics' cross-domain approach asks whether changing a UI parameter, a ranking rule, a constitutional permission, or an underlying paradigm produces radically different leverage.

Reference: Donella H. Meadows, “Leverage Points: Places to Intervene in a System,” Sustainability Institute, 1999.

## Feedback and the kOA capability loop

The canonical kOA cycle:

> **Know → Choose → Act → Remember → Know better**

can be interpreted as a feedback architecture:

```text
state / evidence
→ interpretation
→ decision
→ action
→ result
→ memory
→ revised state / evidence
```

The key Power Dynamics addition is that **feedback channels themselves are power surfaces**. Whoever controls what gets measured, remembered, ranked, or ignored can influence future adaptation.

## Systems caution

A system can be technically adaptive while politically dominating. Cybernetic viability is not identical to legitimacy, justice, or non-domination.

Therefore the systems lineage should be paired with the constitutional lineage rather than used as a substitute for it.
