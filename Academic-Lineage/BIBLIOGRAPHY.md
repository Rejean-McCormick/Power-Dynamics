## Candidates for later expansion

These are significant enough to deserve later treatment but are not yet integrated in detail:

- Hannah Arendt — power as acting in concert, and distinction from violence;
- Pierre Bourdieu — economic, cultural, social, and symbolic capital and conversion;
- Michel Foucault — productive / dispersed power, normalization, and conduct;
- Crozier & Friedberg — organizational power through zones of uncertainty;
- mechanism design / incentive compatibility;
- platform governance and recommender-system power;
- algorithmic accountability and AI-mediated authority;
- common-pool resource design principles in digital infrastructure;
- standards governance and Internet protocol ossification;
- epistemic democracy and deliberative systems;
- institutional diversity and comparative constitutional design.
