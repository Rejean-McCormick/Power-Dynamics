# Formation

Formation is the process through which resources become organized capacity and effective puissance.

Typical transitions:

`information → knowledge`

`practice → expertise`

`money → liquid capacity`

`relationships → network capacity`

`code → technical capability`

`rules → institutional capacity`

`memory → lower future learning cost`

Formation has costs: time, attention, education, infrastructure, coordination, verification, maintenance, and trust. Actors with similar resource stocks can therefore have very different effective puissance.

A central kOA ambition is to reduce the loss between dispersed resources and usable collective capacity.
