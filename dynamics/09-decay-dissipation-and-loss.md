# Decay, Dissipation, and Loss

Power Dynamics must study loss as seriously as accumulation.

Resources and puissance can decay through obsolescence, forgotten knowledge, declining credibility, network fragmentation, depleted finance, technical debt, unmaintained infrastructure, loss of recognition, legal change, language drift, or increasing coordination cost.

**Dissipation** occurs when useful returns are not captured in reusable form. Institutions may repeatedly solve the same problem because experience remains trapped in staff memory, inaccessible archives, or transient conversation.

Decay can also be constitutionally useful. Time decay, sunset clauses, and periodic revalidation can prevent delegated authority or reputation from becoming permanent.
