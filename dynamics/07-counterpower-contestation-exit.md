# Counterpower: Verification, Contestation, Substitution, Exit

Counterpower is practical capacity to alter the terms of a power relation.

> **Verification + Contestation + Substitution + Exit**

- **Verification** — inspect what happened and why.
- **Contestation** — meaningfully challenge the claim, decision, ranking, score, rule, or authority.
- **Substitution** — replace the provider, branch, trust root, policy, or infrastructure.
- **Exit** — leave without disproportionate loss of identity, data, reputation, continuity, rights, or capability.

These are gradients, not checkboxes.

Branch systems add **branch mobility**: an alternative must be able not only to exist but to become discoverable, credible, interoperable, supported, and viable.
