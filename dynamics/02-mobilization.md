# Mobilization: Reservoir, Release, Flow, and Mediation

Mobilization explains how retained resources or existing puissance become externally effective.

> **Reservoir / Retention → Release → Flow / Throughput → Mediation / Amplification → Relation or Effect**

This is **one dynamic inside Power Dynamics**, not the architecture of the whole field.

- **Reservoir** — accumulated resources retained for possible future use.
- **Release** — disclosure, deployment, spending, publication, delegation, or activation.
- **Flow / throughput** — rate at which resources and puissance become action, distribution, coordination, or effects.
- **Mediation / amplification** — selection, ranking, translation, framing, routing, synchronization, and multiplication.

A large unpublished corpus can have high epistemic stock and optionality while producing little public effect until organized, released, distributed, understood, and reused. Media demonstrate the inverse: very high distribution throughput can create large agenda effects.
