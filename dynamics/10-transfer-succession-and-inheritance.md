# Transfer, Succession, and Inheritance

Power and puissance persist across time through transfer mechanisms.

Transfer may involve money, property, credentials, code, repositories, signing keys, administrative roles, authority registries, institutional mandates, brands, knowledge, relationships, and interpretive authority.

Succession is especially important in founder-dependent systems. A system is not independently reproducible if operation, legitimacy, keys, vocabulary, or interpretation remain dependent on one person's unique knowledge or permission.

Distinguish founder authorship from permanent interpretive sovereignty; brand stewardship from protocol sovereignty; maintainer authority from political authority; technical custody from ownership of collective meaning.
