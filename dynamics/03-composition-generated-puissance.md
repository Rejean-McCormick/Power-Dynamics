# Composition and Generated Puissance

Composition combines distinct capacities to create higher-order capability.

`expertise + local knowledge + coordination → project capacity`

`knowledge + translation + distribution → multilingual reach`

`code + governance + deployment → operational infrastructure`

`money + network + technical capacity → implementation capacity`

The resulting **generated puissance** may exist nowhere individually. This is why collective intelligence can become a new pole of capability.

Composition does not require ownership. Independent systems can compose through explicit contracts. The risk appears when the orchestration layer becomes a necessary bottleneck and silently inherits authority over the resources or decisions being composed.
