# Dynamics

The ontology says what a phenomenon **is**. Dynamics describe what **happens to it over time**.

The canonical dynamics are:

1. formation;
2. mobilization;
3. composition;
4. conversion;
5. returns and reinvestment;
6. compounding;
7. concentration, stacking, and capture;
8. counterpower and contestation;
9. diversification and branch evolution;
10. decay, dissipation, transfer, and succession;
11. power externalities and scale transitions.

These dynamics can occur in any domain and at any ontological level.
