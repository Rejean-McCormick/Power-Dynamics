# Dynamic Processes

The Power Show answers **what kOA can make possible**. This directory explains **how capability and power change over time**.

The main dynamic sequence is:

> **Formation → Mobilization → Composition → Conversion → Effects → Returns → Reinvestment → Compounding → Concentration / Contestation → Diversification / Evolution → Decay / Transfer**

## 1. Formation
How resources become potential, capacity, and eventually effectively mobilizable puissance.

## 2. Mobilization
How retained stocks are released, routed, distributed, mediated, amplified, or blocked. `Reservoir → Release → Flow → Mediation` is a mobilization model, not the whole theory.

## 3. Composition and generated puissance
How distinct capabilities combine to create higher-order individual or collective capability.

## 4. Conversion and firewalls
How one resource, capability, or relational advantage becomes another — and where conversion should be constrained.

## 5. Returns, reinvestment, and compounding
How action leaves reusable assets that increase or redistribute future capability.

## 6. Concentration, stacking, and capture
How several legitimate functions or returns accumulate into cross-domain leverage.

## 7. Counterpower, contestation, and exit
How actors alter the terms of power through verification, challenge, substitution, refusal, exit, or fork.

## 8. Diversification and evolution
How variants emerge, demonstrate value, gather support, coexist, recombine, or replace dominant branches.

## 9. Decay, dissipation, and loss
How reputation, network position, knowledge relevance, infrastructure, liquidity, legitimacy, or organizational capacity erode.

## 10. Transfer, succession, and inheritance
How resources, authority, keys, mandates, memory, and stewardship move across actors and generations.

## 11. Externalities and scale
How a useful mechanism can acquire systemic power because adoption, network effects, standards, or infrastructure dependence grow around it.

The sequence is recursive rather than linear. Returns from one cycle become resources for another.
